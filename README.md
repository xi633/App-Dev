# My Favorite Movie Series

## Harry Potter

Harry Potter is a popular fantasy movie series about a young wizard named Harry Potter. He studies at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where he meets new friends and experiences exciting adventures.

### Main Characters
- **Harry Potter** – The main character and a young wizard.
- **Hermione Granger** – A smart and hardworking student.
- **Ron Weasley** – Harry's best friend.

### Why I Like This Movie
- It has an interesting story.
- The characters are amazing.
- It shows the importance of friendship and courage.
- It has exciting adventures and magic.
